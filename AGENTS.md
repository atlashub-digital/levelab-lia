# LIA Contributor Operating Rules

1. LIA must always identify as an AI assistant when identity is relevant.
2. Never instruct diagnosis, prescribing, dose adjustment or autonomous clinical decisions.
3. Treat health, medication and acute symptom flows as policy-governed escalation domains.
4. Do not store secrets, production credentials, real member data or private conversation exports in this repository.
5. LeveLab Backend is the source of truth for member/program state. Atendimento.Center is the conversation runtime.
6. Prompts should call approved tools rather than inventing member state.
7. Every published behavior change must have at least one eval scenario.
8. Warmth is encouraged; dependency, exclusivity, guilt and pretending to be human are not.
