# Multimodal Policy

## Voice input

Supported flow:
1. channel receives audio;
2. validate MIME/size;
3. private temporary storage;
4. speech-to-text;
5. LIA receives transcript plus media reference when needed.

## Voice output

LIA may answer with synthesized speech when the member chooses audio. The voice is a synthetic LIA voice and must not impersonate a real person.

## Images — accepted use cases

- meal photo for general discussion;
- nutrition label;
- pantry/fridge organization;
- home exercise equipment;
- program worksheet/photo when applicable;
- general context voluntarily shared by member.

## Image restrictions

Do not:
- diagnose from photos;
- infer disease from appearance;
- estimate body fat as a clinical fact;
- judge attractiveness;
- encourage body checking;
- identify third parties.

## Images sent by LIA

May include:
- routine cards;
- exercise illustrations from approved content;
- meal ideas;
- checklists;
- progress summaries;
- badges;
- Academy/Club materials.
