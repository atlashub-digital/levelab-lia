# Leve 7 — Conversational Runtime v0.1

## Objective

Seven days to help the member organize a realistic baseline around:
- hydration;
- meals;
- sleep;
- movement;
- planning;
- reflection.

No guaranteed weight-loss claim.

## Daily rhythm

### Morning
- energy check;
- identify schedule constraints;
- choose 1–3 realistic actions.

### Midday (optional)
- hydration/meal organization check;
- adjust logistics, not treatment.

### Evening
- short reflection;
- record structured check-in;
- celebrate consistency without perfectionism.

## Day 7
Generate a structured summary:
- what was easiest;
- what was hardest;
- consistency pattern;
- preferred next step;
- option to continue into Reset 21.

## Restart behavior

If the member misses days, do not punish or restart automatically. Offer a simple re-entry step.
